# Passe Impact Hammamet

## Purpose & Positioning
Passe Impact Hammamet is a targeted employability and civic-engagement platform for students, young graduates, and companies in the coastal region of Hammamet and Nabeul, Tunisia.
It solves the structural disconnect between theoretical academic education and local market needs by:
1. Transforming traditional CVs into a verified, flippable digital ID Card with an "Avatar" (Frontend Dev, IA & Smart Medina, Civic Tech Ta'sir, Hospitality).
2. Rewarding concrete hackathons and field projects in Hammamet with skill points (XP) and direct financial compensation in a local Dinars Tip Jar.
3. Enabling companies and hotel operators to target validated practical competencies directly, bypassing generic resume screening.

## Core Mechanisms
- **Calibrated Onboarding:** A modest initial calibration entry fee (5 DT) signals commitment. In return, members immediately receive a 2 DT welcome compensation credited into their digital Tip Jar, and their profile is flagged as "Ciblé par le Marché".
- **Flippable 3D ID Pass:**
  - *Recto:* Digital ID, current Avatar, XP score, available Tip Jar balance in Tunisian Dinars (TND), real projects.
  - *Verso:* Extracted previous academic history, past CV resume, cryptographic validation hash.
- **Tip Jar & Local Payment Gateways:** Direct support for Tunisian payment systems (Flouci Wallet, Carte E-Dinar D17 La Poste Tunisienne, Konnect, Sobflous).
- **Gamified Hackathons & Challenges:** Local challenges held at iconic Hammamet landmarks (Dar Sebastian, Marina Yasmine Hammamet, Fort de Hammamet) yielding both XP and Dinars rewards.
- **Recruiter Marketplace:** Filterable pool of verified candidates (Students PFE, Junior Workers, Qualified Freelancers) and one-click job/internship matching.

## Users & Roles
- **Étudiants & Chercheurs de Savoir:** Young talents looking for PFE internships, summer jobs, and first employment.
- **Entreprises & Hôtels:** Tech startups, agencies, cultural hubs, and hospitality leaders seeking proven skills.
- **Mentors & Associations:** Civic partners (JCI Hammamet, Enactus, HubAct) leaving verified peer endorsements.
- **Visiteurs:** Browsing guests exploring the platform and guided tour.

## Platform & Technical Scope
- **Stack:** Node.js (Node 24 with native `node:sqlite`), Express REST API, Vanilla ES6+ frontend with Tailwind CSS and responsive design.
- **Data Minimization & Privacy:** Cookie policy limited strictly to session authentication, name, and email. No third-party ad tracking.
